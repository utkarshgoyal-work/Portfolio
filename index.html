02 / A little about me

Equal parts play and precision.

I'm Juno, an independent developer who loves the moment a mechanic clicks. For the past eight years I've worked across gameplay, tools, and design—helping small teams turn ambitious sketches into worlds players want to get lost in.

I care about the details you feel before you notice: a jump with just the right lift, a world that tells its own story, a menu that gets out of your way.
